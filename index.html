About

From schema design to deployment, from UI to infrastructure. No handoff required — the same mind holds the whole system.

Builds for

Every project is an asset. The decisions made early — architecture, data model, design system — compound or they rot. I build to compound.

Craft
over flash.

Clean code, deliberate design, measured choices. Impressiveness is the wrong goal — usefulness and coherence are the right ones.

I'm a full-stack developer and systems builder from Laguna, Philippines. I finished my BSIT with a 1.60 GWA and immediately started building things that mattered — not for a portfolio, but because the problem was real and the solution didn't exist yet.

I draw in graphite and charcoal. I think the same way I draw: start with structure, then refine, then find the one thing that needs to stand out and make it. That's the work, whether it's code or design.

What I'm building now: TindaFlow — a POS system for Filipino small businesses at a price point they can actually afford — and CosCard, a portfolio platform for the Philippine cosplay community. When I'm not building products, I'm doing systems work for clients who need the technical depth to match their operational complexity.
